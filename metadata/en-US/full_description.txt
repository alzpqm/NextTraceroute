NextTraceroute is an Android traceroute app that does not require root access. Enter a domain, IP address or URL to inspect route hops, latency, network and location information.

The app supports IPv4 and IPv6, local trace history, route maps, UDP/TCP DNS and DNS over HTTPS. The interface defaults to Traditional Chinese, with British English available in Settings.

NextTrace and DNS services are operated by third parties and receive the information needed to process your queries. Android 8.0 or later is required.
